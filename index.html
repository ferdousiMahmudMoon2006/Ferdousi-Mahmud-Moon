Smart Helmet Safety System

Developed a smart helmet-based safety system designed to enhance motorcycle rider safety. The system integrates alcohol detection and helmet-wearing detection to prevent the motorcycle from starting when the rider is not wearing the helmet or when alcohol is detected. It also includes an accident detection and emergency alert feature that automatically sends an alert to a predefined emergency contact number in the event of an accident.

Flood Detection System

Developed an Arduino-free flood detection system using a water sensor, LED, buzzer, battery, and switch to detect rising water levels and provide an immediate visual and audio alert.

Smart Stick for the Visually Impaired

Developed a smart walking stick using ultrasonic sensors and a buzzer to detect obstacles and provide audio alerts, helping visually impaired individuals navigate more safely and independently.
